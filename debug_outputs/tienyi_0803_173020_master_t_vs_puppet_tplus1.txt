Conclusion
----------
1. Puppet has left/right 7D Cartesian end-effector poses.
2. Master does not have left/right Cartesian end-effector poses.
3. Master has 7D joint positions and scalar gripper positions.
4. Direct master[t] vs puppet[t+1] Cartesian end-effector comparison is impossible
   without applying the correct FK to master joints.
5. Left gripper generally follows closely except during transitions; right gripper
   has a visible systematic/temporal difference and cannot be described as close
   over the full episode using a 0.05 threshold.
6. Joint-space master[t] and puppet[t+1] are relatively close, but FK is still
   required before making any claim about Cartesian position or rotation error.
